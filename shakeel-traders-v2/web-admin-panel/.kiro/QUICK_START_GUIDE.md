# 🚀 Quick Start Guide: HR & Payroll System

## 5-Minute Setup & Usage

---

## ⚡ Step 1: Set Up Staff (2 minutes)

### Go to: **Users** (in sidebar)

For EACH staff member:
1. Click ✏️ **Edit** button
2. Fill two fields:
   - **Base Salary**: `30000` (monthly salary)
   - **Include in Payroll**: ✅ **CHECK THIS BOX**
3. Click **Save**

**Result:** Staff will now appear in Attendance and Payroll pages.

---

## ⚡ Step 2: Mark Attendance (1 minute daily)

### Go to: **Attendance** (in sidebar)

1. Select today's date
2. Click **"Mark All Present"** button (marks everyone at once)
3. If anyone is absent, click their **Absent** button
4. Click **"Save Attendance"** at the bottom

**That's it!** ✅ Done for the day.

---

## ⚡ Step 3: Record Advance (when needed)

### Go to: **HR & Payroll** → **Salary Advances & Ledger** tab

1. Find the staff member
2. Click **"+ Advance"** button
3. Enter amount and date
4. Click **"Record Advance"**

**Done!** The advance will be deducted from their next payroll.

---

## ⚡ Step 4: Generate Payroll (once per month)

### Go to: **HR & Payroll** → **Monthly Payroll** tab

1. Select month and year
2. Click **"Generate Payroll"** button
3. Review the list
4. When you pay someone, click the green ✅ checkmark

**Done!** Payroll is complete.

---

## 📌 Important Things to Remember

### ✅ Do's:
- ✅ Set base salary for all staff
- ✅ Check "Include in Payroll" for real staff
- ✅ Mark attendance daily (or use "Mark All Present")
- ✅ Generate payroll at month-end

### ❌ Don'ts:
- ❌ Don't worry about unmarked days (auto-counted as present)
- ❌ Don't mark software-only users (uncheck "Include in Payroll")
- ❌ Don't count Friday absences (system ignores them automatically)

## 🔥 Most Common Question

### "Why does it say 'Not set' for base salary?"

**Answer:** You forgot to set the base salary.

**Fix:**
1. Go to **Users** page
2. Click ✏️ Edit for that user
3. Fill **Base Salary** field (e.g., 30000)
4. Check ✅ **Include in Payroll** box
5. Click **Save**
6. Refresh the HR & Payroll page

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use "Mark All Present" every day** - It's faster than clicking each person
2. **Set base salary for everyone at once** - Do it during initial setup
3. **Don't stress about past dates** - You can mark attendance for any date anytime
4. **Export ledgers for records** - Click Ledger → Export to get Excel file
